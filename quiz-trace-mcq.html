W5-6 · Q53Weeks 5–6ConceptVanishing gradient

Why does the vanishing-gradient problem happen?

Hint. Activation gradients are at most 1 (tanh) or 0.25 (sigmoid).
  1. Gradients of traditional activations are in (0, 1].
  2. The chain rule multiplies n of them for early layers → the error signal decreases exponentially with n → early layers train very slowly.
